## 3. Market Notes & Comparability

- **Remote computer health check:** Only one verified fixed‑price remote health check: PC Support UK £45 fixed (duration unspecified). PC Macgicians offers a workshop/collection health check at £45 fixed but with 24–48 hr turnaround, not a one‑hour remote service. PC Macgicians remote support is hourly (£55/hour). Pimlico Computers previously extracted £90 fixed 1‑hour health check but not re‑verified in this pass; treat as lower confidence. Go Remote Support previously extracted hourly‑rate tiers.  
- **Hourly remote IT support:** Verified rates: PC Macgicians remote support £55 per hour (longer sessions), ComputerPro £70+VAT per hour, Go Remote Support previously extracted £39 per 30 min (≈£78/hour) and £69 per 60 min (≈£69/hour). PC Macgicians quick fixes £40 (unspecified duration).  
- **Linux support:** No ad‑hoc remote Linux diagnosis price found. Managed monthly contracts suggest ongoing support is priced per‑server/month. Hourly rates for Linux sysadmin roles from job boards and freelance sites range from £20–£120/hour, with median around £40–£50/hour for contract work. Ad‑hoc specialist rates may be higher (Tiger Computing cites £250–£500/hour, likely for emergency or on‑site specialist work).  
- **Docker/container diagnosis:** No specific advertising; likely falls under general remote IT support or specialist DevOps support, which would command higher hourly rates.  
- **NAS and backup health review:** No specific advertising; could be considered a subset of general IT health check or hardware diagnosis.  
- **Hardware fault diagnosis:** Some providers list hardware diagnosis fees (e.g., PCRepair2000 £60) but these are often on‑site and may include call‑out charges. Remote hardware diagnosis is limited to SMART status, event logs, and visible sensors.  
- **Home/small‑office network diagnosis:** No specific advertising; likely covered under general remote IT support or router‑configuration services, which may be billed hourly.  

Given the adoption of a standard £55/hour rate, the fixed package prices are set as rounded amounts for simplicity and to avoid implying exact multiplication of the hourly rate for longer packages.

## 4. Per‑Package Analysis

### 1. Windows or Linux Computer Health Check (1 hour allowance)
- **Comparables:** PC Support UK £45 fixed (duration unspecified); PC Macgicians workshop health check £45 fixed (24–48 hr turnaround, not directly comparable); PC Macgicians remote support £55/hour; Go Remote Support previously extracted £69/hour (60 min tier); Pimlico Computers previously extracted £90 fixed 1‑hour; ComputerPro £70+VAT per hour.  
- **Fixed launch price:** £55 (rounded fixed price for 1‑hour allowance, including enquiry, preparation, session, reporting and follow-up).  
- **Principal scope‑creep risk:** Customer may expect troubleshooting beyond diagnostics; need to enforce “read‑only” and “no repair” boundaries.  
- **Fixed‑price suitability:** Yes, as the package is diagnostic/report‑only with fixed time.  

### 2. Linux Cleanup and Troubleshooting (1.5 hour allowance)
- **Comparables:** No direct UK fixed‑price remote Linux cleanup found. Closest: ad‑hoc remote IT support hourly rates (PC Macgicians £55/h, ComputerPro £70+VAT/h, Go Remote Support previously extracted £69/hour, £78/hour equivalent) and Linux sysadmin hourly rates (median ~£42/hour from job boards, freelance range £35–£120).  
- **Fixed launch price:** £85 (rounded fixed price for 1.5‑hour allowance, including enquiry, preparation, session, reporting and follow‑up; not an exact multiplication of the hourly rate).  
- **Principal scope‑creep risk:** Cleanup actions (package updates, service restarts, file deletion) require explicit customer approval; risk of scope creep if customer asks for additional changes during the session.  
- **Fixed‑price suitability:** Yes, if the session is strictly limited to agreed‑upon cleanup actions (e.g., package‑cache, temp‑file cleanup) and any further work is quoted separately.

## 7. Risks and Safeguards

- **Scope creep:** Mitigated by strict time‑boxing, written session agreements, and explicit approval for any system‑changing action.  
- **Data‑loss risk:** Require customer to confirm backups before any work that could affect data; use read‑only diagnostics where possible.  
- **Customer expectations:** Clearly state in marketing and session confirmations that the service is diagnostic/report‑only; any repair or fix work is quoted separately.  
- **Market volatility:** Prices should be reviewed quarterly against advertised competitor rates and adjusted as needed.  
- **Liability:** Do not guarantee outcomes; liability is governed by the terms agreed with the customer (see LAUNCH_SCOPE.md).
